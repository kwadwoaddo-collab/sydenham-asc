## Platform

web

## Users
Parents and carers of primary and secondary school children in Sydenham SE26, mostly on phones, often working and short of time. They want to know quickly: is it safe, can you collect my child, what does it cost, how do I book.

## Product Purpose
Sydenham After School Club is a local after-school childcare club (with school drop-off/pick-up) and small-group Maths, English and Science tuition (Primary to A-Level). Success is a parent calling, WhatsApping, booking a free assessment or sending an enquiry.

## Positioning
Childcare and tuition under one roof, with children mixing across schools; tuition built on the Excel programme of study in small groups (max 1:6).

## Capabilities and Constraints
